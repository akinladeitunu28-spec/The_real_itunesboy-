About iTunes

iTunes, popularly known as The Real iTunes Boy, is a Nigerian dancer and digital content creator recognised within the Nigerian student and online dance communities, particularly around TASUED. He is known for energetic dance videos, trending street-dance challenges, Afro-dance routines, street-hop choreography and the popular Mara dance style. Through creativity, entertainment and dance, iTunes continues to build his personal brand and connect with audiences.
